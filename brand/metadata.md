## ArchNest Brand Context

ArchNest is the branded identity for a self-hosted Homarr dashboard. The name combines "Arch" (architecture, structure) and "Nest" (home, organized gathering place). Visual identity uses deep slate/charcoal backgrounds with warm amber or teal accents. Tagline: "Your services, architected."
